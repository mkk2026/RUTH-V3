## 2024-05-18 - Avoid storing high-frequency polling data in root state\n**Learning:** The application was suffering from severe re-renders because `requestAnimationFrame` audio polling was writing directly to `App.jsx` state 60 times a second. Additionally, `socket.on('audio_data')` was doing the same.\n**Action:** Moved the polling logic into children components `TopAudioBar` and `Visualizer` utilizing `analyser` and `socket` refs respectively to ensure the tree only renders when needed.
